AIRLINE PROFILE

About Qatar Airways

Qatar Airways is the flag carrier of Qatar, operating an extensive international network from its hub at Hamad International Airport.

Doha at the Center

Qatar Airways connects passengers through Doha, creating an important bridge between Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas and Australasia.


The airline operates a large fleet of Airbus and Boeing wide-body aircraft, alongside narrow-body aircraft used on selected regional routes.

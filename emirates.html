AIRLINE PROFILE

About Emirates

Emirates is a Dubai-based international airline and one of the world's major wide-body operators.

A Global Aviation Network

Emirates began operations from Dubai in 1985 with just two aircraft. The airline has since developed into a global carrier connecting Dubai with destinations across six continents.


Dubai International Airport serves as the airline's central hub, allowing Emirates to connect passengers between Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas and Australasia.

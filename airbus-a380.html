About the Airbus A380

The Airbus A380 is one of the most recognizable aircraft ever built and the largest passenger aircraft in commercial aviation history.

The A380 made its first flight on 27 April 2005 in Toulouse, France. It entered commercial service with Singapore Airlines on 25 October 2007.

The aircraft features two full-length passenger decks and can carry up to 853 passengers in a maximum-density configuration.

Airbus completed the final A380 delivery in 2021. Although production has ended, A380 aircraft continue to operate with airlines around the world.

A380 Engines

Rolls-Royce Trent 900

One of the two engine options available for the A380. The Trent 900 family powers A380 aircraft operated by several airlines.

Engine Alliance GP7200

The second engine option for the A380. The GP7200 provides approximately 70,000 lbf of thrust per engine.

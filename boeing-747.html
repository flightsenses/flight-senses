About the Boeing 747

The Boeing 747 is one of the most recognizable aircraft ever built. Known around the world as the "Queen of the Skies", the 747 helped transform long-haul air travel and became an icon of commercial aviation.

The first Boeing 747 completed its first flight on 9 February 1969. It entered commercial service with Pan Am in January 1970.

Boeing produced 1,574 aircraft across the 747 family during its production run. The final 747 left Boeing's Everett factory in December 2022 and was delivered to Atlas Air in January 2023.

747-8 Engines

General Electric GEnx-2B67

The Boeing 747-8 is powered by four General Electric GEnx-2B67 high-bypass turbofan engines.

Total Engine Thrust

Each engine produces approximately 66,500 lbf of thrust, giving the aircraft a combined rated thrust of about 266,000 lbf.

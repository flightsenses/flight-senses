🔥 Rolls-Royce Trent XWB-97

The A350-1000 is powered exclusively by two Rolls-Royce Trent XWB-97 engines. Each engine produces up to approximately 97,000 lbf of takeoff thrust, giving the aircraft a combined thrust of roughly 194,000 lbf.

About the Airbus A350-1000

The A350-1000 is the largest member of the Airbus A350 family. With a fuselage nearly 74 metres long, it was designed to provide additional passenger capacity while retaining the efficiency and long-range capability of the A350 family.

The first A350-1000 completed its maiden flight on 24 November 2016 in Toulouse, France. The aircraft received EASA and FAA type certification in 2017 and entered airline service with Qatar Airways in 2018.

The aircraft uses an advanced airframe made with a high proportion of composite and other advanced materials. More than 70% of the airframe consists of advanced materials, including approximately 53% composites.

The A350-1000 combines a 64.75-metre wingspan with advanced aerodynamics and a new-generation propulsion system to deliver efficient long-haul performance.

Trent XWB-97 Engine

Rolls-Royce Trent XWB-97

Developed specifically for the A350-1000, the Trent XWB-97 is the higher-thrust member of the Trent XWB family. It produces up to 97,000 lbf of takeoff thrust.

Total Engine Thrust

With two Trent XWB-97 engines, the A350-1000 can produce approximately 194,000 lbf of combined takeoff thrust.

About Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines is Singapore's flag carrier and one of the world's most recognizable international airline brands.

From Malayan Airways to Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines traces its history to 1947, when Malayan Airways began operations.


In 1966, the airline became Malaysia-Singapore Airlines. In 1972, the company was separated into two airlines, leading to the creation of Singapore Airlines and Malaysian Airline System.


Since then, Singapore Airlines has developed a strong international identity built around its Singapore hub and long-haul network.

Key Differences

What makes these aircraft different?

📏

Size

The 777-9 is longer at 76.7 metres, while the A350-1000 measures about 73.8 metres. The Boeing also has a much wider wingspan when its folding wingtips are extended.

🔥

Engine Power

The 777-9 uses two enormous GE9X engines. Boeing lists approximately 105,000 lbf of Boeing-equivalent thrust per engine.

🌍

Range

Boeing lists the 777-9 at up to 14,820 km, while Airbus lists the A350-1000 at up to 16,850 km.

🪽

Wing Design

The 777-9 uses a carbon-fibre composite wing with folding wingtips. The A350-1000 uses a highly advanced composite wing designed for long-range efficiency.

✈️ Two Different Approaches

The Boeing 777-9 combines a very large airframe, a huge GE9X engine and a folding composite wing. The Airbus A350-1000 focuses on a lighter advanced-material structure, long range and the Trent XWB-97. Both represent the latest generation of large twin-engine long-haul aircraft.

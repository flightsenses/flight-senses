About Lufthansa

Lufthansa is one of Germany's best-known airlines, with a history stretching back to the early days of European commercial aviation.

A Century in the Sky

Lufthansa traces its heritage to 1926, when Deutscher Luft Hansa was created through the merger of Junkers Luftverkehr and Deutscher Aero Lloyd.


A new Lufthansa was established in 1953, with flight operations resuming in 1955. The airline subsequently became an important European operator of aircraft ranging from the Boeing 707 to the Boeing 747 and Airbus A380.

Lufthansa Through the Years

A timeline showing some of the most important moments in Lufthansa's aviation history.

1926

Lufthansa Heritage Begins

Deutscher Luft Hansa was established following the merger of Junkers Luftverkehr and Deutscher Aero Lloyd.

1953

A New Lufthansa

A new company named Deutsche Lufthansa AG was established in preparation for the return of German civil aviation.

1955

Flight Operations Resume

Lufthansa restarted scheduled flight operations, opening a new chapter in German commercial aviation.

Lufthansa entered the jet age with aircraft such as the Boeing 707, transforming its international operations.

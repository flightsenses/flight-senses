🦾 The Heavyweight of the Skies

The Antonov An-225 Mriya was designed to transport extremely heavy and oversized cargo. Its enormous dimensions, six-engine configuration and 640-tonne maximum takeoff weight made it one of the most extraordinary aircraft ever built.

About the An-225 Mriya

The An-225 was developed by Antonov in the Soviet Union during the 1980s. Its original mission was to transport the Buran space shuttle and components of the Energia launch system.

The aircraft first flew on 21 December 1988 and became famous for its extraordinary size and ability to carry extremely heavy and oversized cargo.

The Ukrainian word "Mriya" means "Dream" — an appropriate name for an aircraft that pushed the limits of what was possible in heavy aviation.

🌍 A Global Aviation Icon

During its operational career, the An-225 transported unusual and exceptionally heavy cargo around the world, becoming a symbol of heavy-lift aviation.

Only one An-225 was completed and operated. A second airframe was partially constructed but never completed.

⚙️ Six Engines. One Legend.

The An-225 is powered by six Progress D-18T high-bypass turbofan engines. Three engines are mounted beneath each wing, giving the aircraft the enormous thrust required for heavy takeoffs.

🔥 Combined Engine Thrust

Each D-18T produces approximately 51,600 lbf of takeoff thrust. Across all six engines, the combined takeoff thrust is approximately 309,600 lbf.

🕊️ The End of an Era

The sole completed An-225 was destroyed during the fighting at Hostomel Airport in Ukraine in February 2022. Its loss marked the end of the aircraft's operational career.

Despite its destruction, Mriya remains one of the most recognizable and remarkable aircraft in aviation history.

🔥 The GE9X

The Boeing 777-9 is powered by two General Electric GE9X engines. The GE9X is the largest and one of the most advanced commercial aircraft engines ever developed, featuring a massive 134-inch fan diameter.

About the Boeing 777-9

The 777-9 is the largest passenger variant of Boeing's new 777X family. It combines the long-range capabilities of the 777 family with technologies developed for the 787 Dreamliner.

The aircraft features a new composite wing with folding wingtips. In flight, the wingspan reaches 71.8 metres, while the wingtips fold on the ground to reduce the span to 64.8 metres.

The 777-9 made its first flight on 25 January 2020. The aircraft is currently undergoing flight testing and certification before entering commercial service.

Boeing lists the 777-9 with a typical two-class seating capacity of 414 passengers and a maximum range of up to 14,820 kilometres.

GE9X Engine

General Electric GE9X

The 777-9 uses two GE9X engines developed by GE Aerospace. The engine features a 134-inch (3.4 m) fan diameter and uses advanced materials and technologies designed to improve efficiency.

Engine Thrust

Boeing lists the GE9X-105B1A104 with a Boeing-equivalent takeoff thrust rating of approximately 104,900 lbf. That gives the 777-9 roughly 209,800 lbf of combined rated thrust.

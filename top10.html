Top 10 Powerful Aircraft

The world's most powerful aircraft ranked by combined engine takeoff thrust.

⚙️ How is the ranking calculated?

Total thrust is calculated by multiplying the published takeoff thrust of one engine by the number of engines. Because engine variants can have different ratings, the aircraft configuration used is stated for each entry.

🚀

More engines ≠ always more power

Engine count alone does not determine total thrust. Modern high-thrust turbofans can produce enormous thrust with only two engines.

🔥

GE9X is massive

The GE9X is rated around 105,000 lbf in the 777-9 application, making it one of the highest-thrust commercial turbofans ever produced.

🦅

The six-engine giants

Stratolaunch Roc and An-225 demonstrate how six-engine configurations can create enormous combined thrust.

⚠️ A Note About Engine Variants

Aircraft can be offered with different engines and thrust ratings. This means that rankings can change depending on which certified engine configuration is selected. Flight Senses therefore displays the configuration and thrust value used for each aircraft instead of treating every variant as identical.

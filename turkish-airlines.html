AIRLINE PROFILE

About Turkish Airlines

Turkish Airlines is the flag carrier of Türkiye, operating a large international network from its main hub at Istanbul Airport.

Global Aviation Presence

Turkish Airlines connects Türkiye with destinations across Europe, Asia, Africa, North America and South America. Its Istanbul hub provides a strategic connection point between multiple regions of the world.


The airline operates both narrow-body and wide-body aircraft, allowing it to serve short-haul regional routes as well as long-haul international flights.

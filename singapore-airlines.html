✈️ The Story of Singapore Airlines

Singapore Airlines traces its history back to 1947, when Malayan Airways began operations.

In 1966, the airline became Malaysia-Singapore Airlines. In 1972, the company was divided into two separate airlines, creating Singapore Airlines and Malaysian Airline System.

Singapore Airlines went on to build a strong international reputation and became closely associated with Singapore's aviation industry.

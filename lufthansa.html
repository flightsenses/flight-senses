Aviation History

The first Lufthansa was founded in 1926 following the merger of Junkers Luftverkehr and Deutscher Aero Lloyd.

After the Second World War, a new Lufthansa was established in 1953. Flight operations restarted in 1955, beginning a new chapter in German commercial aviation.

Lufthansa later became an important operator of iconic aircraft including the Boeing 707, Boeing 747 and Airbus A380.
